Acceleration and braking reference

The game model is an F1-style approximation, not claimed telemetry. Acceleration uses engine-limited drive and quadratic aerodynamic drag. At full brake, gameplay now applies a speed-independent 16.2 m/s² deceleration (80% stronger than the reverse control’s 9.0 m/s²) and clamps speed at 0 km/h. Reference acceleration bands are roughly 2.5–2.9 s for 0–100 km/h and 4.5–6.6 s for 0–200 km/h across different high-performance single-seaters. Braking reference distances are about 17 m from 100 km/h and 64 m from 200 km/h; the actual game model outputs are shown below.

Track status

Mandalika is modeled as the 4.301 km, 17-turn Indonesian circuit. “Rwanda Track (Concept)” is a fictional playable concept inspired by Rwanda’s rolling landscape and landmark architecture; the official Bugesera Formula 1 circuit layout is under development, so this is not an official map. Detected self-crossings are converted to elevated bridge decks with a lower rectangular underpass and collision-height separation.
